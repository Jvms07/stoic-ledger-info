Privacy Policy — Stoic Ledger Bot

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Purpose

Stoic Ledger Bot is an internal publishing and workflow automation tool operated exclusively by the owner of The Stoic Ledger YouTube channel (@TheStoicLedgerTV).

2. Data Accessed via Google APIs

The application accesses only the channel owner's authorized YouTube Data, YouTube Analytics, and Google Tasks APIs to schedule video uploads, set custom thumbnails, review channel analytics, and create personal publishing reminders.

3. Data Storage & Sharing

No third-party user data is collected, sold, shared, or transferred. OAuth credentials are stored privately by the channel owner solely to automate channel management.

4. Contact

For questions or data inquiries, contact stoicledger.ops@gmail.com.
